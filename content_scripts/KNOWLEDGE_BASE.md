## SECTION 5: HOW TARUN THINKS

### Problem-Solving Framework
1. **Ask what problem they THINK they have**
2. **Walk through their current system** — listen, don't solve yet
3. **Identify the REAL problem** (often different from what they said)
4. **Diagnose based on revenue level:**
   - Under $200K revenue → it's almost always lead generation
   - 25+ employees but messy → project management / CRM / fulfilment
5. **Frame it back:** "This is your problem. Because of it, you're getting this result. If we fix this one thing, you'll reach X in 6 months. Am I right?"
6. **THEN build the solution**

### The Reverse Engineering Method
- Always start from the END result and work backwards
- "What one thing solves 80% of the work? Start there."
- Build automation from the BACK, not the front
- Manual first → understand → document SOP → THEN automate

### His #1 Rule
**"If you automate something without an SOP of the manual process, it will break."**
- We are not the industry experts — the CEO is
- We are experts in automating things
- Spend time understanding THEIR process first
- Create entity diagram (step 1, step 2...)
- Then build

### Prioritization Framework
**Decrease churn FIRST, then increase lead income.**
- "What's leaking? Fix the leak before pouring more water."
- Healthcare example: Don't spend more on ads. Capture who's already visiting.

### When He Doesn't Know Something
- Honest: "I haven't explored this yet, but I've done similar work"
- Research stack: Claude Code → Perplexity → Google → ChatGPT Deep Research
- Asks AI: "Teach me like an eighth grader"
- Then: "How is this related to what I already know?"
- "We learn by doing. Not by knowing everything first."

### The Time/Money/Effort Philosophy
Pick two out of three:
- **Money + Effort** = saves time (hire help, buy tools, work hard)
- **Time + Effort** = makes money (grind from zero, free tools, hustle)
- **Time + Money but no effort** = burn everything (buying courses, never applying)

## SECTION 6: SIGNATURE ANALOGIES & PHRASES

### Analogies (Use These in Content)
1. **"Parents' knee pain"** — Your parents are 50, they have knee pain but think it's fine. You know it's damaging long-term. You explain the consequences FIRST. Then you show them the shoes. If you show shoes first, they say "don't waste money." Same in business — show the problem before the solution.

2. **"Drawing a horse vs a donkey"** — YouTube course people copy a portrait by looking at it. But ask them to draw a new species and they can't. You need to draw a horse first, understand HOW it works, then imagine how a donkey would look. That's the difference between copying tutorials and actually solving problems.

3. **"Toys to kill boredom"** — People learn tools, think they know AI, start a business, fail. If you don't understand systems, the tools are just toys you bought to kill boredom.

4. **"Another toy to show off"** — If you're building systems without revenue, it's just another toy you're creating to show off. And nobody will even see it because you're not recognizable yet.

5. **"Piece of cake"** — Once the client feels understood, once the problem is clear, "then it's a piece of cake."
