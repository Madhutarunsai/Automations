# Lead Magnet Strategy for @taruns_ai

## The Psychology: Why People Save Things

People DM or comment for lead magnets when:
1. **It saves them hours** — a template they'd have to build from scratch
2. **It feels exclusive** — "my exact setup" not "a generic guide"
3. **It's immediately usable** — not a PDF to read, a FILE to use
4. **It solves a pain they have RIGHT NOW** — not someday
5. **The perceived value is high** — "this is what I charge clients $3,000 for"

## The Rule: Every Lead Magnet Should Be a Working File

Not a PDF. Not a guide. A TEMPLATE they duplicate and use today.

## HOW TO DELIVER THESE

### For DM-gated magnets:
1. They DM the keyword
2. You (or your automation) reply with the link
3. ALSO ask for their email: "Sent! What's your email? I'll add you to my free weekly automation tips."
4. Now you have their email for future campaigns

### For comment-gated magnets:
1. They comment the keyword
2. You reply publicly: "Sent to your DMs!"
3. Send via DM
4. Same email ask

### Automation option (ManyChat or n8n):
- Keyword detection in DMs → auto-send the correct resource
- Log their username + keyword + date in a Google Sheet
- Send follow-up DM 3 days later: "Did you set it up? Need any help?"
