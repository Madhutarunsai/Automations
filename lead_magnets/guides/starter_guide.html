Zero to First Client — The 7-Day Plan

From Nothing to Paid AI Automation Client in One Week — by @taruns_ai

This is the exact playbook I'd follow if I had to start over from scratch today. No audience, no portfolio, no testimonials. Just a laptop and 2-3 hours per day. I used a version of this plan to build to Top Rated Plus with 100% Job Success on Upwork, land 15 staffing firm clients, and hit a $14,840 single project. You can do the same.

Before you start: You need an Upwork account (free), a Gmail address, and access to n8n (free, open source). That's it. Everything else you'll set up along the way.

Day 1 — Upwork Profile Setup

What to do

Build your Upwork profile from zero to "client-ready" using the Upwork Profile Template (see the PROFILE lead magnet for the fill-in-the-blank version).

  1. Headline: Use the format: "I help [niche] [outcome] using [tool] — [proof point]." Example: "I help staffing firms cut hiring time from 6 weeks to 1 week using n8n + AI automation."
  2. Overview (bio): 3 paragraphs — (1) Who you help and what result you deliver. (2) How you do it (tools + method). (3) CTA to message you. Keep it under 200 words.
  3. Skills: Add 15 skills. Mix broad (Automation, AI, Workflow Design) with specific (n8n, Make.com, API Integration, ChatGPT, Claude).
  4. Portfolio: Skip for now — you'll build this on Day 3.
  5. Hourly rate: Set to $50/hr. You'll quote projects, not hourly, but this signals quality.
  6. Profile photo: Professional headshot. Plain background, good lighting. Phone camera is fine.

Time estimate

2-3 hours

Tools needed

Upwork account, the PROFILE template PDF

What "done" looks like

Your Upwork profile is 100% complete. Headline has a specific niche + outcome. Bio mentions at least one result (even if hypothetical: "workflows like these have helped staffing firms cut hiring from 6 weeks to 1 week"). All 15 skill tags are set.

Day 2 — Build Your First n8n Automation

What to do

Build a simple but real automation: Form Submission → Email Notification → Google Sheet Log. This takes 30 minutes and becomes your first portfolio piece.

  1. Install n8n: Run npx n8n or use n8n.cloud (free tier). Open the editor at localhost:5678.
  2. Add a Webhook node: This is your "form submission" trigger. Copy the test URL — you'll use it to simulate form entries.
  3. Add a Gmail node: Connect your Gmail. Set "To" as your own email. Subject: "New Lead: {{$json.name}}". Body: "Name: {{$json.name}}, Email: {{$json.email}}, Message: {{$json.message}}".
  4. Add a Google Sheets node: Create a new Google Sheet called "Leads." Columns: Name, Email, Message, Date. Connect the node and map the fields.
  5. Test it: Use the webhook test URL to send a sample JSON payload: {"name":"Test Lead","email":"test@example.com","message":"I need help with automation"}
  6. Verify: Check that (a) you received the email, (b) the row appeared in Google Sheets.

Time estimate

1-2 hours (including n8n setup)

Tools needed

n8n (free), Gmail, Google Sheets

What "done" looks like

A working 3-node automation that captures a form submission, sends you an email, and logs the lead in a spreadsheet. You've tested it end-to-end and it works.

Day 3 — Record Your First Portfolio Piece

What to do

Record a 3-minute Loom or Vidyard walkthrough of the automation you built on Day 2. This becomes your portfolio video.

  1. Install Loom or Vidyard: Free Chrome extension. Vidyard is free forever; Loom free tier works too.
  2. Script your walkthrough (don't wing it):
Intro (15 sec): "Here's an automation I built that captures leads from a web form, sends instant email notifications, and logs everything in Google Sheets — no code required."

Demo (2 min): Walk through the n8n workflow. Show each node. Trigger the webhook live. Show the email arriving. Show the Google Sheet updating.

Close (30 sec): "This same pattern works for appointment bookings, contact forms, survey responses — any time you need to capture data and act on it instantly. I build these for [your niche] businesses."
  1. Upload to Upwork portfolio: Add the video link, title it "Lead Capture Automation — n8n Workflow Demo", and write a 2-sentence description.

Time estimate

1-1.5 hours (including re-takes)

Tools needed

Loom or Vidyard (free), the n8n workflow from Day 2

What "done" looks like

A clean 3-minute video is uploaded to your Upwork portfolio. It shows a real working automation with a professional walkthrough. You sound confident and clear.

Day 4 — Write Your First 5 Proposals

What to do

Find 5 jobs on Upwork that match your niche and send proposals using the 4-line template.

  1. Search for jobs: Use keywords like "automation," "n8n," "workflow," "AI integration," "make.com," or "[your niche] + automation." Filter to jobs posted in the last 24 hours with fewer than 10 proposals.
  2. Use the 4-line proposal format:
Line 1: Specific observation about their job post (proves you read it)
Line 2: Relevant experience or result ("I built a similar workflow for a staffing firm — cut their hiring time from 6 weeks to 1 week")
Line 3: How you'd approach their project (1-2 sentences)
Line 4: Soft CTA ("Happy to share a quick Loom walkthrough of how I'd set this up. Worth a look?")
  1. Attach your portfolio video from Day 3 to every proposal.
  2. Keep each proposal under 150 words. Clients skim. Short wins.
  3. Send all 5. Don't overthink. Speed beats perfection at this stage.

Time estimate

2-3 hours (including job searching)

Tools needed

Upwork account, the 4-line proposal template

What "done" looks like

5 proposals sent to real jobs. Each is personalized (Line 1 references something specific from their post). Your portfolio video is attached to every one.

Day 5 — Follow Up + Send 5 More

What to do

Follow up on yesterday's proposals that haven't been viewed or responded to. Then send 5 new proposals.

  1. Check proposal status: Go to Upwork → My Proposals → Submitted. See which have been viewed.
  2. For viewed but no reply: Send a follow-up message on the job thread. Keep it short: "Hi [Name], wanted to add — I mapped out a quick approach for this. Happy to share it if helpful." (Don't re-pitch.)
  3. For not viewed: No action yet. Give it 48 hours.
  4. Send 5 new proposals using the same 4-line template. Try a slightly different niche or keyword if yesterday's jobs were thin.

Time estimate

2-3 hours

Tools needed

Upwork account

What "done" looks like

All Day 4 proposals have been followed up on (where applicable). 5 new proposals sent — that's 10 total proposals in 2 days. You're building momentum.

Day 6 — Post Your First LinkedIn Content

What to do

Share what you built this week on LinkedIn. This is your first piece of content — it doesn't need to be perfect, it needs to exist.

  1. Write a post using this framework:
Hook: "I built my first AI automation this week. Here's what happened."

Body (5-7 lines):
- What you built (the form → email → sheet workflow)
- How long it took (be honest — "about 2 hours")
- What it does for a business (saves 30+ min/day of manual data entry)
- One thing you learned
- What you're building next

CTA: "If you're in [niche] and still doing [pain point] manually, DM me 'AUDIT' — I'll show you what to automate first."
  1. Add a screenshot of your n8n workflow as the post image. Visual posts get 2-3x more reach.
  2. Post between 8-10am on a weekday for maximum reach.
  3. Engage: For the next 30 minutes after posting, comment on 5-10 other posts in your niche. This boosts your post's visibility.

Time estimate

1-1.5 hours (writing + engaging)

Tools needed

LinkedIn account, screenshot of your n8n workflow

What "done" looks like

One LinkedIn post is live with a screenshot. You've commented on 5-10 other posts. You've started building your presence — even if you get 0 likes, you showed up.

Day 7 — Weekly Review + Plan Week 2

What to do

Review your first week, track your numbers, and plan next week's actions.

  1. Track your Week 1 metrics:
MetricYour Number
Proposals sent_____ (target: 10)
Proposals viewed_____
Replies / interviews_____
Discovery calls booked_____
Portfolio pieces created_____ (target: 1)
LinkedIn posts published_____ (target: 1)
  1. Diagnose: If 0 replies from 10 proposals — your proposals need work, not more volume. Re-read the 4-line template. If replies but no calls — your follow-up is weak. If calls but no close — your pricing or pitch needs adjustment.
  2. Plan Week 2:
  • Send 10 more proposals (2 per day Mon-Fri)
  • Build 1 more automation (pick from the n8n Workflow Templates guide)
  • Record 1 more portfolio video
  • Post 2-3 times on LinkedIn
  • Follow up on every open proposal
  1. Set a recurring weekly review every Sunday for 30 minutes. Same metrics, same format.

Time estimate

1 hour

Tools needed

Google Sheet or Notion for tracking, your Upwork dashboard

What "done" looks like

You have a completed Week 1 scorecard. You know exactly what worked and what didn't. Your Week 2 plan is written down with specific daily actions.

The 3 rules that got me to Top Rated Plus, 100% Job Success, and $14,840 projects:

1. Time + Money + Effort — pick two. No money? Invest time and effort. No time? Invest money (ads, tools) and effort. There's no shortcut that eliminates all three.

2. Until $10K/month, your only problem is lead generation. Not your skills. Not your tools. Not your pricing. You don't have enough people seeing your offer. Fix that first. Everything else is a distraction.

3. Consistency beats talent. The freelancer who sends 10 proposals every week for 3 months will always beat the one who sends 50 in week one and quits. Show up every day. The math works if you do.
