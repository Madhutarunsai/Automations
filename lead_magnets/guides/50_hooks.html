50 Proven Short-Form Video Hooks

Fill-in-the-Blank Templates + Real Examples from @taruns_ai

Every viral short-form video starts with a hook that stops the scroll in the first 2 seconds. Below are 50 battle-tested templates organized into 5 categories. Each hook includes a blank template you can adapt and a filled-in example using real results.

How to use this guide: Pick 1-2 hooks per video. Fill in the blanks with your own data. The more specific your numbers, the stronger the hook. Record the first line looking directly into the camera before anything else.

Category 1: Origin Story Hooks (1-10)

These build relatability and trust. Use them to share your journey and make the audience see themselves in your story.

#TemplateExample

3"[Time ago] I was [relatable situation]. Today I [impressive result].""Two years ago I was sending cold emails manually for $15/hr. Today I build automations that generate 14,000 leads on autopilot."
4"I almost quit [industry/skill] because of [specific frustration]. Then I discovered [solution].""I almost quit freelancing because of inconsistent income. Then I discovered automation-as-a-service retainers."
5"The moment I realized [old approach] was broken, everything changed.""The moment I realized manual lead follow-up was losing 60% of prospects, everything changed."
6"Here's the truth about [your field] nobody told me when I started.""Here's the truth about AI automation consulting nobody told me when I started."
7"I built my first [deliverable] in [short time]. It replaced [what the client was doing].""I built my first 60-node n8n workflow in one weekend. It replaced 3 full-time employees doing manual data entry."
8"My biggest failure in [field] taught me the one thing courses never will.""My biggest failure in client onboarding taught me the one thing courses never will."
9"I charged $[low amount] for my first [service]. Now I charge $[high amount]. Here's what changed.""I charged $200 for my first automation build. Now I charge $14,840 per project. Here's what changed."
10"[Number] months ago I had zero clients. Today I'm [credential/result].""18 months ago I had zero clients. Today I'm Top Rated Plus on Upwork with 100% Job Success."

How to Use These Hooks

  1. Pick your category based on the video's goal: build trust (Origin), educate (Teaching), stand out (Contrarian), convert (Results), or engage (Question).
  2. Fill in the blanks with YOUR specific data. Vague hooks die. Specific numbers stop the scroll.
  3. Deliver the hook in the first 1.5 seconds. Look into the camera. No intro, no "Hey guys." Just the hook.
  4. Rotate categories so your feed doesn't feel repetitive. Aim for at least 3 different categories per week.
  5. Save your best performers and create variations. A hook that works once will work again with different details.
Pro tip: The hooks with real numbers (Category 4) consistently outperform all others. Always lead with your best metric: 93-98% open rates, 6 weeks to 1 week, 14K leads, 40% to 78% qualified rate. Numbers are scroll-stoppers.
