50 Proven Short-Form Video Hooks

Fill-in-the-blank templates + real examples from AI automation projects

Each hook below has two parts: the template you can customize and a real example showing it in action. Copy, fill in, and post.

Category 1: Origin Story Hooks (1–10)

These hooks build relatability and credibility by sharing your journey.

Hook 1

Template: "I went from [old job/situation] to [new result] in [timeframe]. Here's what changed."

Example: "I went from cold-emailing 200 prospects a week to having a full pipeline of inbound leads in 3 months. Here's what changed."

Hook 2

Template: "Everyone told me [common fear]. They were wrong."

Example: "Everyone told me AI automation was too complex for staffing firms. They were wrong."

Hook 3

Template: "[Time ago] I was [relatable situation]. Today I [impressive result]."

Example: "A year ago I was manually screening resumes until midnight. Today I run a 60-node workflow that sources 14,000 leads on autopilot."

Hook 4

Template: "The moment I realized [old way] was broken, everything changed."

Example: "The moment I realized manual hiring pipelines were broken, everything changed — we cut time-to-hire from 6 weeks to 1 week."

Hook 5

Template: "I almost quit [pursuit] until I discovered [key insight]."

Example: "I almost quit freelancing until I discovered you could reverse-engineer hidden APIs to build automations nobody else could."

Hook 6

Template: "My [person: client/boss/friend] laughed when I said I'd [bold goal]. Then I showed them the results."

Example: "My client laughed when I said I'd cut their ad spend by 70%. Then I showed them 30% of their old budget was generating the same number of clients."

Hook 7

Template: "I spent [time] doing [task] the hard way before I found this shortcut."

Example: "I spent 6 months writing content manually before I found a system that turns a 20-minute voice memo into 42 posts a month."

Hook 8

Template: "Nobody in [industry] was doing this. So I built it myself."

Example: "Nobody in staffing was automating candidate outreach at scale. So I built a system that hits 93-98% email open rates."

Hook 9

Template: "Here's the [dollar amount / time] mistake I made so you don't have to."

Example: "Here's the 3-month mistake I made building workflows by hand so you don't have to — one n8n template could have done it in a day."

Hook 10

Template: "I said no to a [safe option] to bet on [risky option]. Best decision I ever made."

Example: "I said no to a comfortable agency job to bet on AI automation freelancing. Now I'm Top Rated Plus with 100% Job Success on Upwork."
