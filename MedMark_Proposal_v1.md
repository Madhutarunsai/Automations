## 2. What We Are Building (The Flow)

```
LEADS  →  CRM  →  QUOTE  →  ORDER PIPELINE  →  PAYMENT  →  FOLLOW-UP
WhatsApp   Doctors  3-tier   Source → Laser →   Credit     Retention
Site / IG  Hospitals pricing  Pack → Shiprocket  aging +    + churn
Referrals  + history engine                      reminders  tracking
```

One system holds the brain of the business — not your head.

1. **Lead capture** — Website keeps the WhatsApp link (doctors will not
   fill forms). WhatsApp + Instagram + referrals flow into one system
   automatically. No more CSV.

2. **CRM (core)** — Two record types: Doctors/Clinics and Hospitals.
   Each one shows: what they bought, model, date, price tier, past
   issues, who owns the relationship, last contact. This removes the
   "what did he buy 6 months back?" problem and the risk of business
   being tied to one person.

3. **Quotation engine** — Product master (laparoscopy + open surgery)
   with your 3-tier pricing and a compatibility map (telescope ↔ light
   source ↔ CO2 inflator must match). Prescription image → read → match
   → draft quote in your pricing logic → you approve → send.

4. **Order pipeline** — Inquiry → Quote → Confirmed → Sourcing → Laser/
   Pack → Dispatched → Delivered → Payment → Closed. Per-item status so
   you instantly see which single item is blocking an order.

5. **Payment / credit dashboard** — advance %, credit aging
   (15d / 30d / 3mo / 6mo), automatic follow-up reminders.

6. **Follow-up & retention** — post-delivery message branded as MedMark,
   free-repair tracking, and a real churn number (you have none today).

7. **Voice agents (later)** — multilingual, after-hours, payment
   reminders and reactivation calls.

8. **Brand layer (parallel)** — Google Business + Instagram/Facebook
   content so doctors recall MedMark before they buy.

Hub: GoHighLevel + WhatsApp Business API.
